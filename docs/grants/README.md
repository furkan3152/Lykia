# Grants and funding channels (individual developer)

Lykia is built by one developer, with no company and no budget. These are
channels open to individuals. Program terms change: confirm on the official
pages before applying, and claim only verifiable numbers (`GET /v1/stats`,
the `/stats` page).

| Channel | Who runs it | Open to individuals? | What to submit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arc Microgrants | Arc | Yes: small USDC grants for working projects on Arc mainnet. The 2026 round reported 20 grants of 500 USDC and a 14 October close; watch for the next round | The live app, `/stats`, contracts on Arc, a two-minute demo (`docs/pitch/demo-script.md`) |
| Arc and Circle hackathons | Arc, Circle, community | Yes: solo entries are usually allowed | A focused build on Lykia (for example the Fence -> Circle wallet policy compiler, or Marketplace payments inside the Fence) |
| Circle Developer Grants | Circle | The official page speaks of "teams"; no separate individual track is published (checked 2026-11-01). Apply as a solo builder only if the form allows it | [circle-developer-grants.md](circle-developer-grants.md) |
| Circle Agent Marketplace listing | Circle | Yes: a listing, not a grant, but it brings paid x402 traffic in USDC | [../circle/marketplace-listing.md](../circle/marketplace-listing.md) |
| Arc Builders Fund | Circle Ventures | Investment for teams and companies; not a fit today | [arc-builders-fund.md](arc-builders-fund.md) (kept for later) |

What to lead with in every application:

- Built on Arc Network and Circle's products, with the exact products used
  (`docs/circle/README.md`).
- Open source, non-custodial, the server never signs.
- Honest security status: no paid audit, self-review plus fuzz tests
  (`docs/security/self-review.md`), mainnet beta caps because of that.
- Traction only from `/stats`.
