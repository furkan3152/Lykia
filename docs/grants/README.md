# Grant applications (drafts)

Drafts for the owner to complete and submit. Program terms change; confirm them on the official pages before applying.

| Program | Who runs it | Fit for Lykia | Draft |
|---|---|---|---|
| Circle Developer Grants | Circle | Milestone-based USDC funding, technical guidance, co-marketing and mentorship for teams building on Arc and the Circle Developer Platform | [circle-developer-grants.md](circle-developer-grants.md) |
| Arc Builders Fund | Circle Ventures | Strategic investment and ecosystem support for products that need Arc's attributes and build primitives other Arc projects compose with | [arc-builders-fund.md](arc-builders-fund.md) |
| Arc Microgrants | Arc | Small USDC grants for working projects on Arc mainnet. The 2026 round reported a closing date of 14 October; check whether a new round is open | not drafted |
